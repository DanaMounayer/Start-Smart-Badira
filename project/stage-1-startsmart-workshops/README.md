## The idea as it stood at the end of Stage 1

BADIRA presents every assessment as three readings side by side, never combined into one score:

- **Risk** — the screening-priority result
- **Reliability** — how complete the information behind it was
- **Time** — where the assessment sits in the pregnancy

Keeping the three apart was, and remains, the point. An assessment built on partial information is
shown as exactly that.
