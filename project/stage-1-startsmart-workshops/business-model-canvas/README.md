## The nine blocks

Colour carries meaning rather than decoration: **green** for infrastructure (partners, activities,
resources), **plum** for the offer (value propositions), **rose** for the market (relationships,
channels, segments), **clay** for cost and **green** for revenue.

Points worth defending at a pitch:

- **Multi-sided market** — the woman uses BADIRA, the clinic pays for it, the insurer benefits
- **Data is the moat** — the DASH cohorts and RAHMA are the hardest asset to obtain
- **Trust is the retention strategy** — every prediction carries a confidence score
- **No revenue before clinical validation** — we do not sell an unvalidated prediction
